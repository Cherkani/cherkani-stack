# Expert Panels

Cherkani Stack uses expert lenses inspired by the strongest parts of GStack
reviews and engineering lifecycle skills.

## Founder and CEO

Who is this for? What painful job or decision improves? What is the smallest
valuable outcome? What should we deliberately not build?

## Product and design

What user state changes? What is the focal action? What disappears when state
changes? Is the interaction intentional, accessible, and coherent in every
supported theme and language?

## Engineering and DX

What existing pattern should be reused? Where is the trust boundary? What is
the simplest architecture? Can another developer install, run, test, debug,
and resume the work without hidden knowledge?

## QA, security, and operations

What is the failure path? What happens with empty, long, invalid, slow, or
unauthenticated input? Are auth, RLS, secrets, migrations, logs, rollback, and
production approvals covered?

## Independent reviewer

What is overbuilt? What is missing? What assumption is weakest? What would
break in real use? Which finding blocks shipping and which can become debt?

Each panel returns a recommendation, evidence, tradeoffs, confidence, and next
action. Panels advise; goal state and explicit approvals control.
